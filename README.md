# Neon Void

A fast neon space shooter that runs in your browser. One HTML file, no build step, no install, no tracking.

**Play:** https://lukidevpro.github.io/neon-void/

## How to play

- **Move:** mouse, touch drag, or `WASD` / arrow keys. The ship fires automatically.
- **Pause:** `P` or `Esc` · **Sound:** `M` · **Start / restart:** `Space`, click or tap
- Chain kills within two seconds to build a combo multiplier (up to x9).
- Pick up drops: **P** adds a gun, **S** adds a one-hit shield, **+** gives a life.
- Three enemy types: drones, zig-zaggers and armored tanks that shoot back. The swarm gets faster the longer you survive.

Your high score is saved in your browser.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in any modern browser.
